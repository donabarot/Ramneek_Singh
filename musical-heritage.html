Artist's Profile

A life shaped by raag

Carrying the contemplative depth of the Indore Gharana into each performance.

Ramneek Singh has captivated audiences globally, bringing the meditative depth and emotional resonance of the Indore Gharana to premier stages across the world. Her virtuosity has been highly acclaimed at India’s most prestigious classical music festivals, including the historic Harivallabh Sangeet Sammelan, Saptak, Raag Amir in Indore, and the Tansen Samaroh in Gwalior. Managing all artistic and operational aspects of her tours, she has formed a deep emotional bond with listeners through significant performances spanning the UK, Europe, Australia, India, the US, and Canada. Based in Ontario, she serves as a vital bridge for Indian classical music in North America.

As a third-generation torchbearer of Ustad Amir Khan’s visionary legacy, and shaped by the rigorous training of Vidushi Amarjit Kaur and Pandit Vinayak Torvi, Ramneek’s virtuosity spans a rich, multifaceted repertoire. Her performances journey through the gravity of traditional Khayal and Tarana, into the expressive realms of Thumri, Folk, and Sufiana music, frequently incorporating the historic poetry of 13th-century mystic Amir Khusrau.

Beyond traditional classical platforms, Ramneek has engaged in diverse interdisciplinary and cross-cultural collaborations. She collaborated with the Scholastica Ensemble for a unique European music project and lent her voice to Deepa Mehta's acclaimed film Midnight's Children. Expanding her reach into media, she has provided music for Canadian television programs such as Rabbi Noor and Chardi Kala. Her artistry has also been showcased in partnership with major cultural institutions, including the Newark Museum, the Royal Ontario Museum (ROM), the Birmingham Museum, the PAMA Museum, and Toronto’s Harbourfront Centre for the "Music in the Garden" series, among many others.

Furthering her creative pursuits, Ramneek is an accomplished composer and poetess, writing original raag-based compositions and lyrics under the pen name Ibaadat. Her commitment to expanding the classical repertoire was recognized with a Music Creation Projects grant in September 2021, and she continues to innovate with concepts like Healing Melodies: A Journey of Emotions Through Original Indian Classical Compositions for live presentation. Her released albums—Ibaadat, Naad, Gobind Naam, Bandishein, and Saanjh—showcase her expansive repertoire from traditional archival pieces to original compositions, with most of her discography available to listeners worldwide on Spotify and her official YouTube channel.

“A note does not need to be hurried to be powerful, and silence can be as expressive as sound.”

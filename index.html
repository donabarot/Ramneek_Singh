A life in raag

A voice carried by silence, poetry, and raag

Rooted in the Indore Gharana, Ramneek Singh’s music gives each note room to breathe.

Her music is rooted in the Indore Gharana, the tradition pioneered by Ustad Amir Khan, whose spacious, contemplative approach allows a raag to unfold slowly and with deep concentration.

Trained for over a decade under Vidushi Amarjeet Kaur, a senior disciple of Ustad Amir Khan, and later under Pandit Vinayak Torvi, Ramneek carries this heritage into khayal, thumri, bhajan, Gurbani and Sufi repertoire, as well as original compositions written under the pen name Ibaadat.

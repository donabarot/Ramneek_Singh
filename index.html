A voice carried by silence, poetry, and raag

Ramneek Singh's music is rooted in the Indore Gharana, the tradition pioneered by Ustad Amir Khan, an approach that favours spaciousness over speed, and lets a raag unfold slowly, patiently, and with deep concentration.



Trained for over a decade under Vidushi Amarjeet Kaur,, a senior disciple of Ustad Amir Khan, and later under Pandit Vinayak Torvi, Ramneek carries this musical-heritage into khayal, thumri, bhajan, Gurbani and Sufi repertoire and into original compositions written under the pen name Ibaadat.

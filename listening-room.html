The Listening Room

The Poetry of Sound

Explore Ramneek Singh's musical journey through khayal, original compositions, poetry, Gurbani, bhajan, thumri and Sufi music.

Repertoire

Seven forms, one thread of listening

Khayal

The classical heart of the practice — expansive raag development in the spacious, unhurried style of the Indore Gharana.

Original Compositions

New bandishes and songs written under the pen name Ibaadat, carrying the tradition's principles into present-day contexts.

Poetry

Text and melody held as one — a continuation of Ustad Amir Khan's own sensitivity to the word within the note.

Gurbani

Sacred verse rendered with the same contemplative depth and devotion as the classical repertoire.

Bhajan

Devotional song, intimate and direct, drawing the raag's emotional world close to the listener.

Thumri

The lyrical, romantic form — melodic ornamentation in service of feeling and text.

Sufi Music

Music of longing and union, where devotion, poetry and melody meet across traditions.
